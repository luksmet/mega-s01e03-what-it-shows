#4471 is not one issue. Fixing the parser leaves the other two open.

The three problems

parser: the parser reads month first. Every tenant in #4471 writes the day first. The shared parser reads 07/04 as 4 July, so the day and the month swap. This covers app, email and booking-v2 form reports. The fix queued for #4471 is this fix.

hint: booking-v3 tells people to type the month first. The form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People at day-first tenants follow it, and the form reads their input day first. The date flips before the parser sees it, so the parser fix does not reach it. Only booking-v3 form reports belong here.

shift: the date moves by one day. These reports do not swap day and month. The invite lands a day early or late, or shows a different day in another time zone. The desk had already read them as one-day moves (desk-notes.json), but the cause table filed them under parser by tenant. This looks like a time-zone or day-boundary bug. Reporters say they stopped reporting it, so the count is low.

How the reports were placed

  • By tenant, not by intake tag. Intake guesses the tag from the reporter’s browser. The tenant’s locale and booking form decide the cause (causes.ts). The tags disagree with the tenant in both big groups, so the table shows them only for reference.
  • By what happened, not by subject. Several subject lines appear in all three problems. Matching on subject (decision 0001) cannot keep them apart.
  • One-day moves come from the desk’s own reading (desk-notes.json), not from the subject or the tag.

Lessons

  • Before you merge a report into a big ticket, check what the report says happened, not only its subject line.
  • A count is only as good as its key: count by id, and group by tenant, not by the intake tag.
